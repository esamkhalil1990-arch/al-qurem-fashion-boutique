=== LANGUAGES & RTL (CRITICAL) ===

- Full bilingual support: Arabic (default) and English, with a visible AR/EN switcher in the header, persistent across the page.

- Arabic = true RTL: mirror the entire layout direction (nav order, icon direction, alignment, card layouts, button icon placement) using dir="rtl" — not just centered/right-aligned text. English = dir="ltr".

- Use a clean, premium Arabic web font (e.g. "Tajawal", "IBM Plex Sans Arabic", or "Cairo") paired with a clean Latin sans-serif for English (e.g. "Inter" or "Poppins"). Generous line-height and heading hierarchy — must feel premium, not cramped.

- All nav labels, CTAs, section headers, and footer content must exist in both languages and switch instantly without a jarring reload.



=== DESIGN DIRECTION ===

Aesthetic: sophisticated fashion retail — premium, warm, trustworthy, modern. A real boutique brand, not a tech startup.

- Color palette: refined neutral base (off-white / warm ivory / soft charcoal) + ONE confident accent color (deep burgundy, deep emerald, or warm gold/bronze) used sparingly for CTAs and accents. No generic purple-blue AI gradients.

- Typography: strong editorial headings, clear hierarchy, generous letter-spacing on labels.

- Imagery: large, full-bleed, high-quality fashion photography as the dominant visual language (use real-looking stock/placeholder fashion photography — no illustrations, no obviously fake AI imagery).

- Cards: clean rectangular/square product & category cards, subtle hover states (slight image zoom, soft shadow lift) — avoid excessive rounded "bubble" shapes.

- Spacing: generous, confident whitespace between sections.

- Animations: subtle only — fade/slide-in on scroll, smooth hover states, smooth scrolling between anchors. No flashy or gimmicky animation.

- Absolutely avoid: generic AI gradient backgrounds, glassmorphism clichés, cheap-looking stock photos, fake review stars, fake statistics/counters, invented awards, invented product names or prices.
